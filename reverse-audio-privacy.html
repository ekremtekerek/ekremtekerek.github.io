Reverse Audio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Reverse Audio ("the app") records your voice, plays it backwards and scores the reverse singing challenge. It is published by Ekrem Tekerek. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Your recordings stay on your device

The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Recording, reversing, voice effects and scoring all happen on your phone.

  • Microphone is used only while you are recording, after you grant permission.
  • Recordings you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a recording or uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Sharing happens only when you tap Share; the audio file goes to the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your recordings are never shared with advertisers.

The one-time ad-free purchase removes all ads; the ad SDK is then not started at all.

Purchases

The ad-free version is purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we only receive confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your voice.
  • Internet / network state: only for ads and purchase verification.
  • Vibration: for button feedback, can be turned off in settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact
