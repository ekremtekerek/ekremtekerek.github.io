Drawera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Drawera ("the app") teaches drawing with step-by-step lessons that you copy on paper. It is published by Ekrem Tekerek. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

No account, progress stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no user accounts. Your lesson progress, streak, settings and drawing gallery are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Photos of your drawings

  • Camera / photo picker is used only when you choose to take or pick a photo of your drawing, after you grant permission. The app does not read the rest of your photo library.
  • Gallery: photos you save are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • AI drawing teacher (optional): only when you tap "Look at my drawing", that one photo is resized and sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it together with the lesson's reference picture to Anthropic's Claude API to generate feedback in your language. The photo is not stored by our server and is not used to train AI models. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and may keep API data for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • To apply the daily feedback limit, the server stores a random install identifier created by the app and a daily counter for 48 hours. It is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers.

Subscriptions

Premium is purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. To check that a subscription is active, the app sends the purchase token to our server, which asks Google Play for its status; the server keeps only a one-way hash of the token and the result for up to 6 hours. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play at any time.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads, uses no advertising ID and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph your drawing, only when you choose to.
  • Internet: for the optional AI feedback and for Google Play subscriptions.
  • Vibration: for button feedback, can be turned off in settings.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

All personal data lives on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it. Server-side counters expire automatically within 48 hours. For any request, contact us at the address below.

Changes and contact
